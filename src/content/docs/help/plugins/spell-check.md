title: Spell Check
description: Review spelling diagnostics in open documents.
---

Spell Check adds spelling diagnostics and available suggestions to supported open documents. It uses the host's language-service and diagnostics surfaces.

## Correct a word

Enable the plugin, open a supported note and inspect a marked word. Review the suggestions before replacing it, especially for names, technical terms and code-adjacent prose. Check language and dictionary options exposed by your installed version.

Spelling support depends on the configured language resources. This guide does not promise whole-vault scanning or automatic correction of every file.

Use [Markdown Lint](/help/plugins/markdown-lint/) for Markdown formatting rules. A clean spelling result does not validate the meaning or technical accuracy of a note.
